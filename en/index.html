How it works

  1. You send a link

    A link to the video is enough. I can also follow your channel and start as soon as a new video is live.

  2. I make the clips

    I pick the best moments, crop to the speaker and add captions and music.

  3. You approve and publish

    You usually get the clips within 24 hours, weekends included. One round of revisions is included.

What’s in the clips

  • An opening that stops the scroll

    Each clip starts right at the best moment, so viewers stop scrolling in the very first second.

  • Word-by-word captions

    Words appear in time with the speech, and no text ends up under the TikTok or Reels buttons.

  • Cropped to the speaker

    Full-height 9:16 for the whole clip, and the crop follows whoever is talking.

  • Your channel’s style

    Captions, colours and music are matched to your channel’s style. The music is made in-house or licensed.

Frequently asked questions

What do the free samples include?

I make 3 finished clips from one of your videos, the same way as paid clips. They don’t oblige you to buy anything. If you order, you can publish them, and they come on top of your order. If you don’t order, I delete them within 30 days. More details: free sample terms.

Do you need my social media logins?

No. You get the clips as a download link and publish them yourself. If you want me to publish for you, I get a publisher role on your accounts, not your password.

How quickly will I get the clips?

I work every day, weekends included, so you usually get the clips within 24 hours after the video is live or I’ve received the link. The contractual deadline is 3 business days. If you want specific moments, send me the timestamps.

What if I don’t like a clip?

Every delivery includes one round of revisions. Tell me what you’d like changed and I’ll fix the clip. Further rounds of revisions cost €15 per clip. I always fix my own mistakes, such as caption errors, free of charge, and they don’t use up your revision round.

Where does the music come from?

The music is made in-house or licensed. The episode’s own intro music is removed if you don’t own the rights to it. If you want to use a trending sound, you get the clip without music.

Who owns the clips?

You do. You get full rights to the clips once the invoice is paid, and I don’t show them to anyone without your permission.

How long is the contract?

Monthly packages run month to month. If you cancel by the 15th of the month, the service ends at the end of that month; otherwise it ends at the end of the following month.
