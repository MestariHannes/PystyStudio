How it works

  1. You send a link

    Send a link to a YouTube video or a download link to the episode. If you’d rather not send links, I follow your channel and start as soon as a new video is live. I don’t need your login details.

  2. I pick the moments

    I go through the episode using the transcript and pick the moments that work without the rest of the episode. Every clip opens on a face, an emotion or something happening, to give people a reason to stop scrolling.

    If you want specific moments, send me the timestamps (e.g. 12:40–13:15) and I’ll make the clips from those.

  3. I make the clips

    I crop the frame vertically around the speaker and add captions and music. I also level the audio for social media.

  4. You approve and publish

    You get the clips as a download link, usually within 24 hours on business days. One round of revisions is included. You publish the clips yourself, or I publish them for you as an add-on.

What’s in the clips

On screen you see the speaker, the captions and, at the end, a short call to action, such as “Full episode on YouTube”. No titles or text overlays are added, so the clip starts straight on a face or on the action.

  • 1

    Word-by-word captions

    Words appear in time with the speech. White text with a thick black outline stands out against both light and dark backgrounds.

  • 2

    Full-height 9:16 framing

    The crop stays on the speaker or the action for the whole clip, and when the speaker changes, the crop jumps to them. The clip never shows horizontal footage with black bars.

  • 3

    Safe zones

    Text never ends up under the TikTok and Reels buttons or the post description. The same goes for any text already burned into the source video.

Audio and technical details

  • The music is made in-house or licensed, and its drop lands on the clip’s high point. Speech is always heard over the music.
  • Audio at the same level as other social videos (−14 LUFS)
  • Smooth motion and captions (usually 59.94 frames per second)

Frequently asked questions

What do the free samples include?

I make 3 finished clips from one of your videos, the same way as paid clips: framing, captions and music. Samples are made once per customer, and they don’t oblige you to buy anything. If you order, you can publish them, and they come on top of your order. If you don’t order, I delete the samples and the raw files within 30 days. More details: free sample terms.

Do you need my social media logins?

No. You get the finished clips as a download link and publish them yourself on your own accounts. If you want me to publish for you, I get a publisher role on your accounts, not your password.

What kind of videos work for clips?

Videos where the speakers are on camera work best: video podcasts, interviews, webinars and YouTube videos. Vlogs work too. If you don’t have a video ready, in the Pysty Puhe pilot I interview you remotely and make the clips from the interview.

Can I choose the moments myself?

Yes. Timestamps (e.g. 12:40–13:15) or a short description of the moment is enough, and it doesn’t change the price. If you don’t ask for anything specific, I choose the moments myself.

Do I have to send a link every time?

No. I can follow your channel and start as soon as a new video is live. The delivery time then counts from when the video was published. The best option is a shared folder where your finished videos are saved. The original file has better quality. For members-only or private videos, I need the file or a download link. I don’t clip sponsored segments without your confirmation. If a video shouldn’t be clipped, let me know before it goes live.

How quickly will I get the clips?

In as little as a couple of hours, usually within 24 hours on business days, and at the latest within 3 business days. The time counts from when I’ve received the link or the video is live and I have the information I need, such as the sponsored segments. You can get the clips one episode at a time or once a week.

What if I don’t like a clip?

Every delivery includes one round of revisions. Tell me what you’d like changed and I’ll fix the clip. Further rounds of revisions cost €15 per clip. I always fix my own mistakes, such as caption errors, free of charge, and they don’t use up your revision round.

Where does the music come from?

The music is made in-house or licensed, and no copyrighted music is used in the clips without permission. The episode’s own intro or background music is removed if you don’t own the rights to it. If you want to use a trending sound on TikTok or Instagram, you get the clip without music.

What if the episode is sponsored content?

Then the clip is also labeled as an ad. The disclosure goes in the post copy and uses the platform’s own tool (Branded content or Paid partnership), so no text is added to the video. If you publish yourself, I’ll tell you how to add the disclosure. I don’t clip sponsored segments without your confirmation.

Who owns the clips?

You get full rights to use the clips once the invoice is paid, and you can edit them freely. The music is made in-house or licensed. I don’t show your clips to anyone else without your permission.

Who is the service for?

For businesses and entrepreneurs with a business ID (such as a VAT number or company registration number): YouTube creators, podcasts, coaches and experts who publish long-form video regularly.

How long is the contract?

Monthly packages run month to month, with no minimum contract. Cancel by email: if you let me know by the 15th of the month, the service ends at the end of that month; otherwise it ends at the end of the following month. If you start mid-month, the first month’s price and number of clips are prorated by day. The single batch, the Pysty Puhe pilot and the AI ad package are one-off jobs with no monthly contract.
