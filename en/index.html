How it works

  1. You send a link

    Send a link to a YouTube video or a download link to the episode. If you’d rather not send links, I follow your channel and start as soon as a new video is live. I don’t need your login details.

  2. I pick the moments

    I go through the episode using the transcript and pick the moments that work without the rest of the episode. Every clip opens on a face, an emotion or something happening, so people stop scrolling.

    If you want specific moments, send me the timestamps (e.g. 12:40–13:15) and I’ll make the clips from those.

  3. I make the clips

    I crop the frame vertically around the speaker, add captions and add music. The audio is leveled for social media.

  4. You approve and publish

    You get the clips as a download link. One round of revisions is included, and you publish the clips yourself on your own accounts.

What’s in the clips

On screen, there’s the speaker, captions and a short call to action at the end, such as “Full episode on YouTube”. No titles or text overlays are added, so the clip starts straight on a face or on the action.

  • 1

    Word-by-word captions

    Words appear in time with the speech. White text with a thick black outline stands out against both light and dark backgrounds.

  • 2

    Full-height 9:16 framing

    The crop stays on the speaker or the action for the whole clip, and when the speaker changes, the crop jumps to them. Horizontal footage with bars is never used at any point.

  • 3

    Safe zones

    Text stays clear of the TikTok and Reels buttons and the post description. The same goes for any text already burned into the source video.

Audio and technical details

Music
The music is made in-house or licensed, and its drop lands on the clip’s high point. Speech is always heard over the music.
−14 LUFS
Audio is normalized to the usual social media level, so your clip doesn’t sound quiet next to other videos.
59.94 fps
Clips are rendered at 59.94 frames per second, so motion and captions run smoothly.

Frequently asked questions

What do the free samples include?

You get 3 free sample clips before you decide. The samples are for evaluation: you can publish them once you order. If you don’t order, I delete the samples and the raw files within 30 days. More details: free sample terms.

Do you need my social media logins?

No. You get the finished clips as a download link and publish them yourself on your own accounts. If you want me to publish for you, I get a publisher role on your accounts, not your password.

What kind of videos work for clips?

Videos where the speakers are on camera work best: video podcasts, interviews, webinars and YouTube videos. Vlogs work too. If you don’t have a video ready, in the Pysty Puhe pilot I interview you remotely and make the clips from the interview.

Can I choose the moments myself?

Yes. Timestamps (e.g. 12:40–13:15) or a short description of the moment is enough, and it doesn’t change the price. If you don’t ask for anything specific, I choose the moments myself.

Do I have to send a link every time?

No. I can follow your channel and start as soon as a new video is live, and the delivery time then counts from when the video was published. The best option is a shared folder where your finished videos are saved, because the original file has better quality. For members-only or private videos, I need the file or a download link. I don’t clip sponsored segments without your confirmation, and if a video shouldn’t be clipped, let me know before it goes live.

How quickly will I get the clips?

Delivery usually takes 1–2 business days from when I receive the link or the video goes live. You can get the clips one episode at a time or once a week. Rush delivery within 24 hours costs 50% extra. If a rush delivery is late for reasons on my side, the rush fee is refunded.

What if I don’t like a clip?

Every delivery includes one round of revisions. Tell me what you’d like changed and I’ll fix the clip. Further rounds of revisions cost €15 per clip. I always fix my own mistakes, such as caption errors, free of charge, and they don’t use up your revision round.

Where does the music come from?

The music is made in-house or licensed, and no copyrighted music is used in the clips without permission. The episode’s own intro or background music is removed if you don’t own the rights to it. If you want to use a trending sound on TikTok or Instagram, you get the clip without music.

What if the episode is sponsored content?

Then the clip needs a disclosure too. It goes in the post text and uses the platform’s own label (Branded content or Paid partnership), so the video itself stays clean.

Who owns the clips?

You get full rights to use the clips once the invoice is paid, and you can edit them freely. The music is made in-house or licensed. Your clips are not shown to anyone else without your permission.

Who is the service for?

For businesses and entrepreneurs with a business ID (VAT number): YouTube creators, podcasts, coaches and experts who publish long-form video regularly.

How long is the contract?

Monthly packages run month to month, with no minimum contract. Cancel by email: if you let me know by the 15th of the month, the service ends at the end of that month; otherwise it ends at the end of the following month. If you start mid-month, the first month is invoiced pro rata by day. The single batch, the Pysty Puhe pilot and the AI ad package are one-off jobs with no monthly contract.
