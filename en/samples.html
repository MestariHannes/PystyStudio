Free samples: how they work

Updated 23.10.2026

  1. I make 3 free sample clips from one of your videos. Samples are made once per customer, and they do not oblige you to buy anything.
  2. By sending the link, you confirm that the video belongs to you or your company and that you have the right to give it to me for making the samples. You also confirm that the people appearing in the video accept its use.
  3. The samples are for evaluation. You may publish them once you order a monthly package or a single batch. They are then included in the first delivery.
  4. I use your video only for making the samples. I do not pass it on to anyone, and I do not show the samples to others without your permission.
  5. If you do not order, I delete the samples and the raw files made from the video 30 days after I sent you the samples.
  6. Services are for businesses only. The terms of an order are set out in the agreement you receive before we start.
